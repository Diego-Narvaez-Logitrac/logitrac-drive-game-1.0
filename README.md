# Logitrac Drive

Logitrac Drive is a standalone, portrait 2D mobile arcade project from Logitrac, built in
Unity 6.3 LTS (6000.3.29f1).

## Current status

The Games shell is built. The Games screen shows four game cards, and each card opens a
preview with the game's description, round length, mode, controls and how to play.
Gameplay isn't built yet: pressing Play on a preview shows a Coming Soon panel.

## Games

The images below are concept art, not gameplay.

### Convoy Rush

![Convoy Rush concept art](Assets/Art/Thumbnails/Blue%20Hour%20Convoy%20Rush.png)

*Concept art.* Drive around an arena collecting cars that join your convoy. Avoid walls,
traffic and your own tail. Drag to steer. Endless, 30–90 s rounds. Score: cars collected
plus time survived.

### Highway Blitz

![Highway Blitz concept art](Assets/Art/Thumbnails/Logitrac%20midnight%20highway%20blitz-3.png)

*Concept art.* Dodge traffic on a three-lane highway. Swipe to change lanes and chain
near misses for bonuses and combos. Endless, 45–120 s rounds. Score: distance plus
near-miss bonus.

### Precision Garage

![Precision Garage concept art](Assets/Art/Thumbnails/Silver%20Logitrac%20Van%20in%20Precision%20Garage-1.png)

*Concept art.* Park a vehicle in its marked space without touching cones, walls or other
cars. Draw a path and the vehicle follows it. 20–90 s levels, rated 1–3 stars for no
contact and speed.

### Turbo Circuit

![Turbo Circuit concept art](Assets/Art/Thumbnails/Logitrac%20GTs%20Charge%20Through%20the%20Apex-2.png)

*Concept art.* Mini racing on tight tracks against rivals and the clock. The car
accelerates on its own; hold left or right to steer, and hold both to drift and fill
your boost. 60–180 s races. Score: lap time.

## How to run it

1. Open the project with Unity 6000.3.29f1.
2. Open `Assets/Scenes/Games.unity`.
3. Set the Game view to 390 × 844.
4. Press Play.
